About Us

Pickle.ai is a pickleball club built to help players play more, compete better and connect with each other.

Our story

Play. Compete. Connect.

Pickle.ai was established in August 2026 with one goal: to build a connected pickleball network across Noida, NCR and Uttar Pradesh.

We organise tournaments, leagues and DUPR nights, run bootcamps for schools and societies, and partner with venues so players always have a court to play on. Everything we do is about bringing players, venues and events together in one place.
